# Day 15 — Shared service catalog

[Previous day](day-14.md) · [Plan](../learning-plan.md) · [Next day](day-16.md)

**Goal:** Describe services once, then let many professionals offer them.

- [ ] Learn: taxonomy is shared reference data; a category groups service types, while a professional's industry describes their field.
- [ ] Agree whether one service may have multiple categories. Add admin-managed service types and a public active-service list.
- [ ] Predict duplicate slug and inactive-service results; test them. Keep provider-specific price and description out of the shared catalog.

**Done when:** Public visitors can list valid service types and only admins can change the catalog.
